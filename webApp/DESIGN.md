# NotePad web direction

Visual thesis: a quiet paper journal in warm off-white, graphite, and one
terracotta mark, with the ink canvas as the visual anchor.

Content plan: the notebook rail orients the user, the paper workspace is where
the work happens, the details rail holds typed text and paper settings, and the
backup/sync controls keep recovery visible without becoming a dashboard.

Interaction thesis: the rail slides in on small screens, the paper enters with
a short lift, and tool/status changes use restrained color and motion so the
surface feels responsive while a reduced-motion preference stays calm.
